Responsible AI: System Limitations & Verification

  • Nyquist Ceiling: The DSP filter cannot synthesize harmonic information that was physically lost or unrecorded above 8 kHz on historic 16kHz transfers.
  • Honest Transcription: If non-vocal music or field percussion is detected, speech generation is strictly suppressed to avoid hallucinations.
  • PII Protection: Personal contact information is masked prior to Dublin Core serialization.
